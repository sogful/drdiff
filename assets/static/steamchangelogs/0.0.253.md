# DELTARUNE Chapter 5 v0.0.253 Patch

Today we released an update for PC/Mac and PlayStation to fix a few issues with the game. You can confirm the chapter version number on the bottom right of the file select screen.

### Chapter 5 Version 0.0.253

### ADDITIONAL CHAPTER 5 FEATURES
- After losing to the Shadow Crystal boss, a Pink Bomb will appear outside the arena. Talking to this bomb allows you to choose "Nicer" or "Meaner". Choosing "Nicer" causes the boss's small bomb attacks to have an indication of where their explosions will target. The bombs will also take 1/6th of a second longer to explode. Choosing "Meaner" causes all of the boss's attacks to deal 25% more damage as well as slightly increases the speed of a couple attacks. The bomb explosion hitbox also activates 1 frame later regardless of difficulty.*

_* Note: Girls like it when you are better at dodging danmaku._

- Malius now animates when fusing items.

### FIXED BUGS
### Chapter 2
- Removed a softlock caused by disconnecting an audio device during sequences synced to the music.
- Losing against Queen no longer fails to replenish your used items.
- A Downed character will no longer stay Downed after defeating Queen.

### Chapter 3
- Fixed a softlock involving getting the Power Croissant and saving Lancer in Board 1 in a specific order.
- Fixed 2 softlocks involving turning enemies into pushable objects.
- Attempted to fix a crash involving Kris being downed when Tenna's final attack starts.

### Chapter 4
- Fixed an issue where Susie's magic was erroneously upgraded to BetterHeal upon fighting the boss of the Second Sanctuary. This change is also reflected in Chapter 5 - Susie's healing will no longer be improved if you didn't defeat the ShadowCrystal boss. (Although the name of the spell is the same either way now)
- You are no longer prevented from getting the BlackShard from the cliff if you have the BlueRibbon.
- Spamton's box will no longer appear at the Cliff.
- You can no longer avoid getting chased before meeting the Old Man for the first time.
- When fighting the Shadow Crystal boss, the button prompt that appears should now properly reflect the button you are supposed to press.
- You no longer lose TP from quitting out of Susie's menu selection when fighting the boss of the 2nd Sanctuary.
- Removed a softlock caused by disconnecting an audio device during the credits.
- Fixed a few minor issues.

### Chapter 5
- Fixed a softlock introduced by a previous bug fix in the Garden room with the light pillar.
- Fixed a crash introduced by a previous bug fix where you could defeat Yellow with violence using a precise set up.
- Fixed a bug where you can walk around with the dialogue box open in the room where you fight Sheary.
- Removed Honey Toast from the inventory when sleeping in the bed at the beginning of the Chapter.
- Fixed dozens of minor bugs and visual issues.

_Thanks for your patience while we adjusted the game. We will now return to focusing on developing Chapter 6. We have no current plans for another patch unless any additional issues arise._
