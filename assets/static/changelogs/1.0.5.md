# DELTARUNE Chapter 1&2 1.0.5 Changelog

### Made it more convenient after getting a Game Over in certain locations
- You can choose to give up after losing to the last boss of the chapter, going back to your last save. (Before it just said "Don't Give Up!")
- Losing to Queen brings you to the same room with your HP restored.
- Losing to the hard teacup ride respawns you in the same room.
- Losing to the hidden boss puts you right BEFORE you inserted the disk, so you can go back.
- Fixed various other crashes, softlocks, bugs, and details.
