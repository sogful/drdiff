# DELTARUNE Hotfix Patch

We are releasing a hotfix for PC & Mac to remedy some issues occurring in the game. You can confirm the version number on the bottom right of the file select screen.

### Ch3 v.0.0.089
- Fixed crashes relating to Shadowguy's Sharpshoot ACT.

### Ch4 v.0.0.088
- Removed key combinations that caused various issues across the game.
- Fixed a crash relating to talking to an NPC when it's raining.
- Strengthened imagery while maintaining a scene's intended meaning.
- Fix of trapped out the closet.

> We will begin the process of issuing the previous patches (1.01A and 1.01B) to consoles soon. The next patch will take longer and address non-fatal bugs. After that, we will take some more time to consider other adjustments.
